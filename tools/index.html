Tools

Hey! Alex here, these are just some tools for helping you work with conlangs or worldbuilding projects. I still have no idea what other tools to add, so if you have any ideas pleasseeeeee message me on the discord. Anyway, enjoy them :3

Word Generator

word-generator.conlangatucsd.com

Describe your sounds, pick a syllable shape, and get words that follow your rules. Every setting is stored in the page link, so you can bookmark a generator or send it to a friend.

Open word generator

Dictionary Manager

dictionary.conlangatucsd.com

Keep your conlang's words in one place. Add, edit, search, and filter entries entirely in your browser, then export a Tlag-compatible JSON or CSV file to share or back up. Try it with the built-in Tlag sample dictionary.

Open dictionary manager

Sound Change Applier

phonology.conlangatucsd.com

Write historical sound changes in IPA, run them on words, and see each rule apply step by step.

Open sound change applier
