Alienable and inalienable possession

Alienable

  • Objects
  • Friends
  • Communicable disease
  • Property

Inalienable

  • Body parts (e.g. corpse, hair)
  • Family members
  • personal qualities (e.g. age, criminal status, name, gender, emotions, personality, mental illness)
  • Incommunicable disease
  • Past Actions/Deeds/Utterances/Impact
  • Invention/Creation/Conlang/Art/Residue

Other features

  • No wh-movement
  • No agreement
  • Null copula
  • Pro-drop
  • Adverbs used to form predicative adjectives (like in Russian and Tamil)
  • No infinitive
