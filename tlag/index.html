Our club conlang

Tlag

Tlag, pronounced /tɬæŋ/ (the closest we could get to "clang" with the tlag phonology) is conlang club's club-wide language, worked on and iterated by all members, meant to be passed down through our club's hopefully rich future.

How it's made

Essentially all we do to write tlag is talk about it during meetings or on our discord, and if we come to a consensus, it's changed! No other hassle, we're extremely informal when it comes to changing or adding to tlag, since we're descriptive rather than perscriptive.

7 phonological rules

  1. Nasal place assimilation
  2. Schwa deletion before non-high vowels
  3. Consonant deletion before same consonant
  4. Vowel lengthening in open syllables
  5. Vowel lengthening before voiced consonants
  6. Retroflex assimilation of /tsʼ/
  7. Syllable-final h-deletion

Morphology & syntax

Show summary

Word order

VSO in main clauses, SVO in subordinate clauses. Noun first before adjectives; determiners, numerals and possessors come before the noun.

Noun cases

  • cina nominative, no ending
  • cinahv definite accusative, -hv
  • cinamic genitive, -mic
  • cinaibi ablative / instrumental, -ibi

Verb forms

  • caam non-past
  • ucaam past, u-
  • nicaam irrealis, ni-/nij-
  • icaam imperative, i-/ij-
  • caamus active participle, -us
  • caamek passive participle, -mek
  • caamir gerund, -ir/-jir

Possession split

Alienable: objects, friends, property. Inalienable: body parts, family, personal qualities, creations and past deeds.

Other features

  • No wh-movement
  • No agreement
  • Null copula
  • Pro-drop
  • No infinitive

Dictionary

906 entries: mostly nouns (597) and verbs (108), searchable by Tlag or English and filterable by part of speech and category such as food & drink, organisms, and kinship.

Pronouns

First, second and third (animate/inanimate) persons with singular, paucal and plural numbers. Includes inclusive/exclusive “we” and sentence-final tone tags like ne (jocular) and jaa (sarcastic).

Particles & affixes

Enclitics such as diminutive -uvu, intensifier -e/-je and causative -va; bound morphemes such as adjectivizer -xeu, verbalizer -leg, agentive -paali and negation -(j)ol.

Numbers

Base-4 leaning system: itli 1, ku 2, tipi 3, naape 4, naapitli 5 (lit. “four one”), ikaapaa 8, tuhu 10.
