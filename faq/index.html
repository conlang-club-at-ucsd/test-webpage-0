FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the questions we get most often.

Do I need a linguistics background to join?

No. All majors and experience levels are welcome. Curiosity about language is the only prerequisite. Our workshops start from the basics and build up.

What happens at a typical meeting?

Weekly workshops, critique circles for works-in-progress, and collaborative work on our club conlang, tlag. Bring a notebook or laptop if you want to work hands-on; otherwise just come to listen and ask questions.

When and where do you meet?

We meet on Fridays between 1:30 and 5:00 in various Geisel study rooms

How do I join?

Just join the Discord and drop by a meeting; no application. You can also email us at conlangclubatucsd@gmail.com with questions.

Can I showcase my own conlang on the site?

Yes! Officers and members can be featured on the Member Conlangs page. Share a short description and a sample phrase in Discord and we'll add you.

Is there a membership fee?

There's no fee to join or attend meetings. We want to be as inclusive as possible!

How do I contact the officers?

Reach us via Discord or email. See the Officers page for roles and individual conlang projects.

Still have a question?

We're happy to help. The fastest way to get an answer is Discord.
