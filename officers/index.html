I'm Sina, president of Conlang Club. I've been a conlanger for roughly 10 years now, working on a conlang now called Ravya. I started Ravya as a language intended to be spoken by llamas in a science fiction book I wanted to write when I was in 5th/6th grade; through that I developed a love for linguistics and have been making that conlang ever since!

Ravya is one of my favorite things to talk about, but I'm also huge nerd about a buncha other stuff like world history, culinary history, food science, vexillology, photography, zoology, physiology, and almost everything else under the sun. Please reach out to me whenever, for conlanging reasons or otherwise :D

Hi! I'm the main developer for conlang club-related things, like this website. I'm a prolific conlanger, with over 30 full (ish) conlangs under my belt. My favorite is Aurathil (which by the way, you can see on the member conlangs page), and its Wind-Sensed Evidential.

I'm also a huge fanatic for worldbuilding, and have been working on my own world for a couple years now! I'm usually making Minecraft mods or writing music, but occasionally you can see me doing low-level things in c. I've also made a couple of programming languages, including one that I call Invisible Ink, because all of the characters you write are either zero-width or whitespace utf-encoded characters.
